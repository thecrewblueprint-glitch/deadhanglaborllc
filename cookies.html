Cookie Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026

1. What Are Cookies and Browser Storage

Cookies are small data files that websites can place in a browser. Browser storage, including localStorage and sessionStorage, is a related technology that stores information locally in your browser.

This policy describes both technologies so visitors can see exactly what this website currently uses.

2. What This Website Uses

The current website uses one item of browser storage:

  • Cookie notice acknowledgement (localStorage key: cookieAck) — When you dismiss the informational cookie notice, the site stores the value locally so the notice does not reappear on later visits. The value records only that the notice was dismissed. It is not used to identify you and is not transmitted to Deadhang Labor LLC as part of the site code.

The current site code does not set first-party tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or session-identifying cookies.

3. What This Website Does Not Currently Use

The current production site does not implement:

  • Google Analytics or other visitor analytics platforms
  • Advertising or retargeting cookies
  • Social-media tracking pixels
  • Heatmapping or session-recording tools
  • Live-chat tracking widgets
  • Marketing automation or email-list tracking
  • A/B testing tools
  • CRM tracking pixels

4. Third-Party Services and Technical Requests

Google Fonts

Typography is delivered through Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests font resources from Google. Those requests may include standard network and browser information such as IP address, browser details, referring information, and the requested resource. See Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's practices.

CloudFront-Hosted Media

Many site and portfolio images are delivered from a cloudfront.net content-delivery domain. Loading those images requires your browser to make network requests to that infrastructure, which may involve standard request information such as IP address, browser information, referring page, and requested file.

Web Hosting and Server Logs

The website's hosting environment may maintain standard access and security logs for technical operations, availability, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention. Deadhang Labor LLC does not use those logs for behavioral advertising.

5. Contact Links

The Contact page currently uses direct mailto: links rather than a web form. Clicking an email link may open your email application or email provider. Any cookies or storage used by your email service are controlled by that service and are not set by deadhanglaborllc.com.

6. Your Choices

You can clear the cookieAck acknowledgement at any time by clearing site data or local storage for this domain in your browser. Clearing it causes the notice to appear again on a later visit.

You may also configure your browser to restrict cookies or browser storage. The site's public content and direct email links are designed to remain usable without the acknowledgement value being stored.

7. Future Changes

If analytics, advertising, social integrations, or other tracking technologies are added in the future, this Cookie Policy should be updated before or when those technologies are activated, as required by applicable law.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when website functionality, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The Effective Date above reflects the latest material revision.
