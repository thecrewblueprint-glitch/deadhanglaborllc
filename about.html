About

Deadhang Labor LLC

Live event production labor. Hands-on experience. Clear communication.

Who I Am

I’m Aaron Bowman. Deadhang Labor LLC is my company, and it’s how you hire me. I’m a stagehand, and I work calls anywhere in the country.

I got my start as a local hand in 2024, working arena and stadium calls. I came in with a construction background, so building things with my hands already felt natural. As I proved myself, I started getting called for corporate events too. About a year in, I started traveling to build shows away from home, and I’ve been stacking up festival runs across the country ever since.

What I Like Doing

I’m proud of everything I’ve built with my coworkers and friends. The people I work with have become like family to me, and the bonds that form in this industry are a big part of why I love it. At the end of the day, we build beautiful things for people to come be amazed by, and they leave with happy memories.

Building scaffold and decking is my favorite part of the job. I love watching the vision take shape, going from empty space to the finished build. As a production hand, I really enjoy video walls and lighting. I like staying busy and moving. The days go by faster and my energy stays up.

The road is where I want to be: new places, new crews, bigger builds.

Field Experience

Staging and structures, scaffold and decking, load-in and load-out, LED video walls, lighting, audio support, follow spot and RoboSpot, and set changes during shows.

I travel for work, from arena and stadium shows to large multi-day festivals.

How I Work

I listen to the lead, communicate clearly, and get the task done. If something doesn’t add up halfway through, I’ll come find you instead of guessing.

If something is unsafe, unclear, or not my call, I stop and get the right person.

Learn by Teaching

I make a point of understanding what we’re building and why. That way, when someone on the crew gets confused, I can walk them through it, and explaining it keeps me sharp too. When I’m sent out with a team, I get the plan straight first, then get everyone moving on it.

Learn by Teaching

Understand it, then explain it. Every call adds to what I know.
