Company profile

An operating business built from doing the work.

Deadhang Labor LLC is Aaron Bowman's owner-operated production-labor business. The company exists to make hands-on live-event experience easy to understand, contract, document, and build on professionally.

The business profile is intentionally narrower than the productions shown in the portfolio. Deadhang provides labor and support within assigned scope; it does not claim authority simply because it has worked around complex systems or major events.
Aaron Bowman / owner-operator

Field experience first. Business structure around it.

Deadhang grew out of stagehand work across festivals, arenas, stadiums, touring productions, and other live-event environments. The operating principle is straightforward: arrive prepared, understand the assignment, respect the lead structure, work safely, communicate clearly, and keep the call moving.

The website is not meant to turn that work into a larger title than it was. Its job is to show the environments worked, the practical capabilities developed, and the type of production support Deadhang can bring to a future call.

Development

Experience widened by doing more kinds of calls.

The progression is less about collecting titles and more about becoming useful in more environments while keeping the limits of each role clear.

Foundation

General stagehand work

Load-ins, load-outs, equipment movement, stage setup, backstage coordination, and the basic habits required to function inside a professional production crew.

Department exposure

Lighting, audio, video, scenic, and staging support

More calls around specialized departments developed better system awareness while preserving the difference between supporting a department and holding specialist authority.

Large venues

Arena, stadium, and show-call experience

Work in larger rooms added tighter timing, access constraints, communication requirements, and assigned spotlight responsibilities.

Travel

Festivals and multi-state production work

Travel assignments added site logistics, long-duration calls, outdoor conditions, changing schedules, housing/travel coordination, and repeated work with rotating crews.
