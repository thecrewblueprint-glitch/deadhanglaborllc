About

Deadhang Labor LLC

Live event production labor. Hands-on experience. Clear communication.

Who I Am

I’m Aaron Bowman. Deadhang Labor LLC is my company, and it’s how you hire me. I’m a stagehand based in Arizona, and I work calls anywhere in the country.

I got my start as a local hand in 2024, working arena and stadium calls. I came in with some construction background, and it wasn’t long before I was getting trusted with corporate calls too. About a year in, I started traveling to build shows away from home, and I’ve been stacking up festival runs across the country ever since.

What I Like Doing

I’m proud of everything we build. At the end of the day it’s a place where a lot of people come together, and I got to put my hands on it.

Building scaffold and decking is some of my favorite work. As a hand, video walls and lighting are where I’d rather be. I like staying physically busy; a day on the move goes by fast.

I’m glad to take arena and stadium calls when I’m home, but the road is where I want to be: new places, new crews, bigger builds.

Field Experience

Staging and structures, scaffold and decking, load-in and load-out, LED video walls, lighting, audio support, follow spot and RoboSpot, and set changes during shows.

My work has taken me through Arizona, Michigan, Ohio, Virginia, Florida, California, Wisconsin, and other markets.

How I Work

I listen to the lead, communicate clearly, and get the task done. If something doesn’t add up halfway through, I’ll come find you instead of guessing.

I stay heads up the whole shift. Builds have natural lulls while forklifts move and areas clear, but that’s no reason to stop paying attention to what’s moving around you. I look out for my teammates the same way I look out for myself.

If something is unsafe, unclear, or not my call, I stop and get the right person.

Learn by Teaching

When I understand what’s going on, I can explain it to the hands around me when there’s confusion, and teaching it keeps me sharp too. When I’m sent out with a team, I figure out what we’re building and get everyone moving on it.

Learn by Teaching

Understand it, then explain it. Every call adds to what I know.
