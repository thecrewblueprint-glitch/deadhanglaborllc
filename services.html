Services / working scope

Useful labor starts with a clear assignment.

Deadhang supports established production teams with hands-on field labor. Scope is defined by the call, department needs, site rules, equipment, access, and the applicable lead structure.

This page describes work Deadhang can support. It does not replace task-specific supervision, venue rules, qualified-person requirements, engineering authority, or production management.
01 / STAGING

Staging & structural support

Hands-on support around stage decks, barricade, scenic elements, temporary production systems, movement, build, and strike.

  • Deck and stage-system support
  • Barricade and field layout labor
  • Scenic movement and build support
  • Strike and restoration support
Boundary

Support work does not create engineering, rigging, structural-design, or inspection authority. Specialized tasks remain with the appropriately qualified lead or specialist.

02 / LOGISTICS

Load-in & load-out

Production-floor and truck labor designed to keep equipment moving cleanly through the call.

  • Truck unload and reload
  • Road-case and equipment movement
  • Department sorting and staging
  • Strike, pack, and dock support
Working approach

Follow the truck lead, department lead, venue traffic plan, and established equipment-handling requirements.

03 / DEPARTMENTS

General production support

Labor around established audio, lighting, video, staging, scenic, backline, and related departments according to assignment.

  • Cable, case, and equipment movement
  • Department prep and strike support
  • Basic assembly tasks under direction
  • General show-site labor
Boundary

Department support is not a claim of electrician, engineer, programmer, system designer, rigger, or department-head authority.

04 / SHOW CALL

Spotlight operations

Assigned follow-spot and spotlight work where the position, equipment, access, cues, and show expectations are established.

  • Pre-show position and equipment checks
  • Show-call cue execution
  • Communication with the applicable lead
  • Strike or position closeout as assigned
Requirement

Work depends on appropriate training, safe access, equipment condition, show requirements, and site-specific authorization.

Travel work

Travel is part of the business when the call makes sense.

Phoenix is the operating base. U.S. assignments are considered when the schedule, work scope, travel plan, lodging, meals/per diem or other arrangements, and compensation are clear in advance.

Preferred planningDates, venue/city, role, call times, expected duration, travel days, lodging, and compensation provided before confirmation.
Longer travel callsFor travel-based assignments, one week or more is generally preferred unless another arrangement makes operational sense.
Travel arrangementsAirfare, ground travel, lodging, meals/per diem, or another agreed structure may be used depending on the assignment.
ConfirmationAvailability is not final until the assignment terms and expected scope are understood.
