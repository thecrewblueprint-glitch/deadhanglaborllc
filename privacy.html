Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026

1. Who We Are

Deadhang Labor LLC ("Deadhang Labor," "we," "us," or "our") is an Arizona limited liability company providing independent live event production labor and related production support throughout the United States. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit deadhanglaborllc.com or contact us using the email links provided on this website.

2. Information You Provide

This website does not currently operate a web contact form and does not submit inquiry data to a form-processing service. The Contact page provides direct mailto: links that open your email application or email service.

If you choose to email us, we receive the information you include in that message. Depending on what you send, that may include your name, email address, company or organization, event or production details, dates, location, schedule, message content, and any attachments you choose to provide.

Please do not send payment card information, government identification numbers, medical information, or other sensitive personal data unless it is specifically necessary for an established business relationship and an appropriate secure method has been agreed upon.

3. Technical Data Generated When You Visit

Like most websites, limited technical information may be processed as part of normal delivery and security operations. This may include IP address, browser or device information, requested pages or files, referring page, and request timestamps.

Deadhang Labor LLC does not currently use analytics platforms, advertising pixels, behavioral profiling tools, heatmaps, or session-recording software on this website.

4. How We Use Information

Information you voluntarily send to us is used for legitimate business purposes such as:

  • Responding to production and availability inquiries
  • Evaluating potential engagements and scheduling
  • Communicating about contract, call, travel, or production requirements
  • Maintaining ordinary business records and correspondence
  • Protecting the website, business, and communications from misuse or fraud

We do not sell or rent personal information for advertising or marketing purposes, and we do not add people to marketing lists merely because they contact us.

5. Direct Email and Third-Party Services

When you click an email link on this website, the message is handled by your chosen email application or provider and by the email service used by Deadhang Labor LLC. Those services process communications under their own privacy and security terms. The website itself does not intercept or store the contents of your email before it is sent.

This website also relies on limited third-party infrastructure to deliver content:

  • Google Fonts — Typography is requested from Google's font servers. Those requests may expose standard connection information such as IP address, browser information, and requested resource data to Google. Google's privacy information is available at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • CloudFront-hosted media — Many portfolio and site images are delivered from a cloudfront.net content-delivery domain. Requests for those images may transmit standard network and browser request information to the content-delivery infrastructure.
  • Web hosting provider — The hosting environment may maintain standard server and security logs for availability, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and technical operations.

6. Cookies and Browser Storage

This website does not currently set tracking, advertising, or user-profile cookies. It uses a single browser-storage entry, localStorage key cookieAck, solely to remember that the informational cookie notice has been dismissed. That value is stored locally in your browser and is not used to identify you.

See the Cookie Policy for additional detail.

7. Data Security

The website is served over HTTPS. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the information we handle. Email security and transmission also depend on the email providers used by the sender and recipient. No internet transmission, email system, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Email correspondence and related business records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for active or potential engagements, recordkeeping, dispute resolution, legal compliance, tax or accounting needs, or other legitimate business purposes. Information that is no longer needed may be deleted in the ordinary course of business.

9. Your Privacy Requests

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held directly by Deadhang Labor LLC. To make a privacy request, email aaron@deadhanglaborllc.com with the subject line "Privacy Request." We will review the request and respond as required by applicable law.

Requests concerning information held independently by your email provider or another third-party service must be directed to that provider.

10. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for business and professional use in the live-event production industry and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly use this website to solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact aaron@deadhanglaborllc.com.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in website functionality, business practices, third-party services, or legal requirements. The Effective Date above will be revised when this policy is materially updated.

12. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the State of Arizona, without regard to conflict-of-law principles, except where another jurisdiction's law applies as a matter of law.
