Who I Am

Deadhang Labor LLC is my independent live event production labor company. I’m Aaron Bowman, a Phoenix-based stagehand and production worker available for projects across the United States.

My experience comes from the field: arenas, stadiums, festivals, touring productions, corporate events, warehouses, and outdoor builds. I’ve worked across staging, structures, load-in and load-out, audio, video, lighting support, spotlight, and general production.

Field Experience

I’ve worked with ground support and staging systems including G2, Tait, PGP Blue Steel, and Layher, along with large-format video systems and production equipment across multiple departments.

My work has taken me through Arizona, Michigan, Ohio, Virginia, Florida, California, Wisconsin, and other markets. Festival and event credits include Electric Forest, Lost Lands, Country Thunder, Innings Festival, Hondo Rodeo Fest, and Reggae Rise Up.

How I Work

I listen to the lead, communicate clearly, and do the work in front of me.

I know what I know. If I don’t know something, I ask. I learn quickly, pay attention, and don’t guess when the answer matters.

If something is unsafe, unclear, or not my decision to make, I stop and get the right person involved.

Deadhang Labor

Deadhang Labor exists to provide dependable production support where it is needed. The work comes first: show up prepared, understand the assignment, communicate, and support the crew.

I’m available for festival runs, touring work, arena and stadium calls, corporate production, and other travel-based assignments when the schedule and arrangements make sense.
